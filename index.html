Meet The Founder

Creating Greater Access To STEM For Girls

STEM Sister was created by Rhia Singh, an aspiring Biochemical Engineer who believes every girl deserves the opportunity to see herself as a scientist, engineer, researcher, innovator or leader.

Through resources, challenges and positive role models, STEM Sister aims to remove barriers, build confidence and inspire young women to explore opportunities within STEM fields.

"You don't need to fit a stereotype to belong in STEM. Your curiosity is enough."
