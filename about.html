The Story Behind STEM Sister

Turning Curiosity Into Purpose

STEM Sister began with a simple question: Why aren't more girls encouraged to see themselves as future scientists, engineers and innovators?

From a young age, Rhia was fascinated by science, discovery and technology. She loved exploring how things worked and how new ideas could improve people's lives.

As her interests developed, she became especially inspired by Biochemical Engineering, a field that combines biology, chemistry and engineering to solve real-world problems through healthcare, biotechnology and sustainability.

While exploring STEM careers, Rhia noticed a challenge that still exists today: many STEM industries remain heavily male dominated.

Too many talented girls never pursue STEM simply because they don't see themselves represented or encouraged in those fields.

"Every girl deserves the opportunity to dream, create, innovate and lead."
