Hi, I'm Rhia Singh, founder of STEM Sister.


I believe every girl deserves the opportunity to dream big, explore boldly and discover her full potential.


STEM Sister was created because I wanted to build a safe, welcoming and inspiring platform where girls can access learning resources, explore careers and gain confidence.


My goal is simple: every girl who visits STEM Sister should leave believing, "I belong in STEM."

Why STEM Sister Exists

Around the world, girls remain underrepresented in many STEM careers despite having the creativity, intelligence and determination needed to thrive.


STEM Sister exists to provide encouragement, opportunity, inspiration and practical resources that help girls discover exciting pathways into STEM.
