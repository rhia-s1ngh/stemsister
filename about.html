About STEM Sister

STEM Sister was created to help girls discover their potential, embrace curiosity, build confidence, and explore exciting opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics.

👩🏽‍💻 Meet Rhia Singh

Hi, I'm Rhia Singh, founder of STEM Sister.

I've always believed that education has the power to transform lives. Along my journey, I realised that many girls have incredible potential but often lack access to the encouragement, opportunities and resources needed to fully explore their talents.

STEM Sister was created as a place where girls can learn, explore, ask questions, develop confidence and discover the exciting possibilities available within STEM.

My dream is for every girl who visits STEM Sister to leave feeling inspired, capable and excited about her future.

Why STEM Sister Exists

Around the world, girls remain underrepresented in many STEM careers despite having the talent, creativity and determination needed to succeed.

STEM Sister exists to create a supportive environment where girls can access learning resources, develop confidence, gain inspiration from role models and discover pathways into STEM.
