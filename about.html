About STEM Sister

Empowering girls through education, technology, confidence, and opportunity.

Meet The Founder

👩🏽‍💻 Rhia Singh


Hi! I'm Rhia Singh, an Indian South African woman who is passionate about STEM, education, technology, and creating opportunities for young girls around the world.


I created STEM Sister because I believe that every girl deserves access to quality education, regardless of where she lives or her circumstances. Knowledge has the power to change lives, build confidence, and unlock endless possibilities.


Through STEM Sister, I hope to inspire girls to dream big, learn new skills, explore science and technology, and become the innovators, engineers, scientists, entrepreneurs, and leaders of tomorrow.

💜 Our Mission


To provide free, accessible, engaging educational resources that empower girls to build confidence, develop STEM skills, and create brighter futures for themselves and their communities.
