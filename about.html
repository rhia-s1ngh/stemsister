👩🏽‍🔬 The Story Behind STEM Sister

Meet Rhia Singh


STEM Sister began with a passion for discovery.


Rhia Singh has always been fascinated by science, innovation and the idea that one person can create solutions that make a real difference in the world.


As her interest in STEM grew, she became especially interested in Biochemical Engineering, a field that combines science and engineering to improve healthcare, biotechnology, sustainability and human wellbeing.


While exploring these opportunities, Rhia noticed that many STEM industries remain heavily male-dominated. Talented and capable girls often hesitate to pursue STEM careers because they don't always see themselves represented in those spaces.


She believed that had to change.


STEM Sister was created to encourage girls to pursue their interests without limits, challenge stereotypes and gain confidence in their abilities.


Her mission is simple:


Every Girl Belongs In STEM.
