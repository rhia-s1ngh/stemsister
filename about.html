Meet The Founder

Turning Curiosity Into Purpose

STEM Sister was founded by Rhia Singh, an aspiring Biochemical Engineer with a passion for science, innovation and creating opportunities for young women.

As she explored STEM careers, Rhia became increasingly aware that many girls still struggle to see themselves represented in scientific and technical fields.

She created STEM Sister to help change that by making STEM more accessible, inspiring and welcoming for every girl who is curious enough to ask questions, solve problems and pursue big ideas.

"Every girl deserves the opportunity to dream, create, innovate and lead."

A Message From Rhia

When I created STEM Sister, my goal was simple: to help more girls realise that their ideas, creativity and curiosity belong in STEM.


Whether you dream of becoming a scientist, engineer, doctor, entrepreneur or innovator, I hope this platform encourages you to take the first step and believe in your potential.
