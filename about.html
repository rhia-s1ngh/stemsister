Meet Rhia Singh

Turning Curiosity Into Purpose

STEM Sister began with a simple belief: every girl deserves the opportunity to dream bigger, explore boldly and pursue ambitious goals.

As an aspiring Biochemical Engineer, Rhia developed a passion for science, innovation and solving real-world problems.

While exploring STEM careers, she noticed that many girls still struggle to see themselves represented within scientific and technical fields.

STEM Sister was created to help change that, providing inspiration, resources and support for future scientists, engineers and innovators.

"Every girl deserves the opportunity to dream, create, innovate and lead."
