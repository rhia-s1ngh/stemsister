Meet The Founder

Turning Curiosity Into Purpose

STEM Sister began with a simple belief: every girl deserves the opportunity to dream big and pursue her interests without limitations.

Founder Rhia Singh developed a passion for science, technology and innovation, particularly within the field of Biochemical Engineering.

While exploring STEM pathways, she noticed that many girls still struggle to see themselves represented in these fields.

STEM Sister was created to help change that. The platform encourages girls to explore, learn, innovate and believe in their ability to shape the future.

"Every girl deserves the opportunity to dream, create, innovate and lead."
