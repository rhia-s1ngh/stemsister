About STEM Sister

STEM Sister was created to inspire, support, and empower girls to believe in themselves, embrace learning, and confidently explore opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.

Meet The Founder

👩🏽‍💻 Rhia Singh

Hello and welcome to STEM Sister! My name is Rhia Singh, a proud South African woman with a passion for education, technology, innovation, and creating opportunities for others.

Throughout my journey, I have witnessed the incredible potential that young girls possess when they are given access to learning, encouragement, and support. Yet many girls still face barriers that prevent them from seeing just how far they can go.

STEM Sister was born from the belief that every girl deserves a space where she feels capable, confident, inspired, and valued. A space where questions are encouraged, curiosity is celebrated, and dreams are transformed into goals.

My hope is that STEM Sister becomes more than a website. I want it to become a community that helps girls discover their strengths, unlock their potential, and realise that there is a place for them in every STEM field imaginable.

Why STEM Sister?

Around the world, girls remain underrepresented in many STEM careers despite having the talent, determination, and creativity needed to succeed.

Many young girls never receive the encouragement, resources, mentorship, or opportunities needed to pursue their interests confidently. STEM Sister aims to help bridge that gap by offering educational resources, interactive learning opportunities, wellbeing support, and positive role models.

We believe every girl deserves the chance to dream big and to know that she belongs in laboratories, boardrooms, classrooms, research centres, engineering firms, technology companies, and wherever innovation happens.
