# 🌟 STEM Sister

STEM Sister is an educational platform created by Rhia Singh to inspire, empower, and support girls in exploring opportunities within Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM).

## 💜 Our Mission

STEM Sister was built on the belief that every girl deserves the opportunity to discover her potential, develop confidence, and pursue her passions without limitations.

Our mission is to make STEM education accessible, engaging, and inspiring while encouraging more girls to participate in fields where women have historically been underrepresented.

## 🚀 Why STEM Sister?

Many STEM industries continue to be male-dominated, and young girls often lack the visibility, encouragement, and support needed to confidently pursue STEM careers.

STEM Sister aims to help bridge this gap by providing:

- 📚 Learning Resources
- 🧪 STEM Challenges and Activities
- 🌍 Career Exploration Tools
- 🌟 Inspirational Women in STEM
- 💬 Community Engagement
- 💜 Wellness and Confidence Building

## 🔬 What STEM Means

STEM stands for:

- **Science** – Understanding the world through discovery and experimentation.
- **Technology** – Creating innovative digital solutions.
- **Engineering** – Designing and building solutions to real-world problems.
- **Mathematics** – Developing logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

## 👩🏽‍🔬 Founder Story

STEM Sister was founded by **Rhia Singh**, a passionate advocate for STEM education with a particular interest in **Biochemical Engineering**.

Inspired by the potential of science and engineering to improve lives and solve global challenges, Rhia created STEM Sister to encourage more girls to pursue careers in STEM and feel confident entering industries where women remain underrepresented.

Her vision is simple:

> "Every girl should know that she belongs in STEM."
